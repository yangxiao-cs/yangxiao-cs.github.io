Dr. Yang Xiao (the last name is Xiao) is a professor of the Department of Computer Science at The University of Alabama. He is an IEEE fellow, an IET Fellow, and an AAIA Fellow. His research areas include Cyber-Physical Systems, Security, Internet of Things, Wireless Networks, Smart Grid, and Telemedicine.
